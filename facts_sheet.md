# Nexa Group - Internal Service Desk Facts

## Password Reset

Employees can reset their Nexa Group password through the company
password portal.

The password portal is available from the Nexa Group employee
intranet.

If an employee cannot access the password portal, the IT service
desk should be contacted.

## VPN Access

Remote employees who need access to internal Nexa Group systems
must use the approved Nexa Group VPN.

VPN access requires an active employee account.

Employees who cannot connect to the VPN should contact the IT
service desk.

## Software Requests

Employees can request approved software through the internal IT
service desk.

Software requests may require manager approval depending on the
software requested.

## Leave Policy

Employees should use the Nexa Group HR portal to submit leave
requests.

Leave approval depends on the applicable company policy and the
employee's manager.

The service desk should not invent additional leave rules.

## Remote Work

Employees should follow the current Nexa Group remote-work policy.

The service desk can provide general information about the policy
but should escalate questions requiring an HR policy decision.

## Out-of-Scope Requests

NexaDesk provides internal IT and HR policy assistance.

It should not provide medical, legal, financial, investment, or
other unrelated professional advice.

For questions outside its supported scope, NexaDesk should clearly
state that it does not have that information and offer to connect
the employee with a human agent.
